Main assumptions of the Lifetime Warranty

The warranty covers the entire offer

All Lionelo products are covered by a Lifetime Warranty. It covers manufacturing defects and those that occurred during proper use.

Voluntary registration

The product must be registered at lionelo-protect.com within 90 days of purchase. If it is not registered within this period, the product is still covered by a 5-year warranty.

Applies only to the first buyer

The warranty applies only to the person who first purchased the product for private use. The warranty is non-transferable and does not pass to other persons.
